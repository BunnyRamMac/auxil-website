# Auxil SEO Keyword Strategy

Last updated: 2026-08-11

This strategy maps real Auxil offerings to search intent without invented volume, doorway pages, or duplicate canonical targets.

## Principles

- One search intent maps to one canonical page.
- Service pages must explain a real offer, not repeat city or keyword variants.
- Product pages must avoid claiming public availability before launch.
- Location pages publish only when Auxil has genuine business relevance and unique content for the location.
- Titles and descriptions should be accurate, human-readable, and free of unsupported best/#1/leading claims.

## Cannibalization Rules

- `/consulting` owns AI consulting, AI development, AI automation, and product engineering discovery intent.
- `/enterprise-services` owns US staffing, US recruitment, RPO, technical recruitment, payroll, and workforce management intent.
- `/products` owns product and branded product discovery. Resume-builder intent is mapped under CareerSignal Global as a career intelligence capability, not as an independent Auxil product.
- `/locations/hyderabad` owns only Hyderabad-local intent tied to Auxil's real headquarters context.
- `/locations` is a hub and policy page, not a broad ranking target for every geography.

## Location Decisions

| Location | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Hyderabad | Publish `/locations/hyderabad` | Auxil is headquartered in Hyderabad. The page can describe real operating context, relevant services, product work, internal links, FAQ, CTA, and valid Service/Breadcrumb/FAQ schema. |
| India | Do not publish a dedicated page yet | India is already reflected through headquarters/company context. A standalone India page would risk duplicating company and consulting content unless Auxil has unique India-specific services, offices, case studies, or hiring information to publish. |
| United States | Do not publish a dedicated page yet | United States relevance exists through US staffing and recruitment services, which are better owned by `/enterprise-services`. A US location page would be thin without a verified US office, address, team, or unique market content. |
| Other cities | Do not evaluate further now | No current evidence of real local operations, unique service content, or business relevance that would justify city pages. |

## Future Expansion Criteria

A new location page should publish only when at least three of these are true:

- Auxil has a verified office, operating base, delivery team, or recurring client/service context in the location.
- The page can include unique services or constraints relevant to that market.
- The page has a distinct H1, title, meta description, FAQ, internal links, and CTA.
- The content helps users decide whether Auxil is relevant in that location.
- Schema can be applied truthfully without fabricating address, reviews, ratings, clients, or metrics.
